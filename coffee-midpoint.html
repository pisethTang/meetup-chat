Each spot is ranked by both travel times. Pick a spot to see both routes on the map. Blue is your route, orange is your friend's. Dotted lines are walking, or estimates when no route was found.

Pick "Meeting at a set time" and each person's time counts from when they need to leave home, so a bus that gets in 20 minutes early counts against that spot. Driving times allow for weekday peak-hour traffic.

Public transport trips come from Transitous, using Adelaide Metro timetables (no live delays). Addresses, road times, cafés and free car parks come from OpenStreetMap when the page can reach it. If not, it uses the suburb in each address and a built-in list of cafés. Check parking signs on the day.
