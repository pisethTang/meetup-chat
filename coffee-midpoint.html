    How it works

    Each spot is ranked by both travel times. Tap a spot to see both routes on the map. Blue is your route, orange is your friend's. Dotted lines are public transport or straight-line estimates.

    Addresses, road times, cafés and free car parks come from OpenStreetMap when the page can reach it. If not, it uses the suburb in each address and a built-in list of cafés. Public transport times are estimates. Check parking signs on the day.
